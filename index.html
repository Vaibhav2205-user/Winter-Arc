IRON WEEK
The weekHow to progress

Three days. Twice. Then rest.

Chest with triceps, back with biceps, legs with shoulders. Run the rotation Monday to Saturday, take Sunday off. Every muscle gets trained twice a week.

How to progress

Reps first

Pick a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with clean form. Add a rep each week until every set hits the top of the range.

Then load

Once every set hits the top, add the smallest jump you have (2.5 kg on barbells, 1 to 2 kg on dumbbells) and start the range again.

Stop one or two short

Leave one or two reps in reserve on most sets. Go to failure only on the last set of isolation moves. Back off for a week every six to eight weeks.

Recover on purpose

Sleep seven to nine hours, eat enough protein across the day, and warm up with two light ramp sets before your first heavy lift.

Training guide for healthy adults. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain, and see a professional for injuries or medical conditions.
